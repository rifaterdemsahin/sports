🎯 Why sleep is part of 75 Strong

The rule of thumb is one hour of sport a day. That hour at 06:35 only happens if you are in bed early enough. Target ~7.5–8 hours: lights out 22:00–22:15, alarm ~05:45, creatine + snack 06:15, Parkside from 6.30am.

🕗 Weeknight wind-down

  1. 🍽️ Dinner with carbs if you trained (tennis Wed, gym mornings).
  2. 🫒 EVOO still fine at dinner (vault: 1 tbsp/meal).
  3. 📱 Screens dim after 21:30.
  4. 😴 If you use them: L-Tryptophan (“L-Trotoplan”) and/or melatonin gummies — from Jun 2025 vault.
  5. 🧲 Extra magnesium (2 caps, vault) is often evening; don’t stack new doses without your own notes.
  6. 🛏️ In bed by 22:15. CB1 1BB → Parkside is a short walk; don’t steal sleep for a long commute.

📅 By day

  • Sun–Tue, Thu night: early bed — 06:30 Better next morning.
  • Wed: tennis until ~19:15. Eat after, still aim 22:30 latest. Don’t skip Thursday Reading sleep.
  • Thu (Reading): no 06:30 gym. Keep a normal bedtime anyway so Friday swim isn’t wrecked.
  • Fri: optional table tennis. If late, Saturday tennis is 10:00 (Parkside also 8.00am–5.30pm).
  • London work nights: hotel/dark room; same 22:00 target. Morning walk is the hour — still sleep first.
