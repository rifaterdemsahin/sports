2. ⚠️ Hypoglycaemia Protocol Check

  1. Pre-fueling: 15–30 min prior snack (banana/toast/juice). Never train fasted.
  2. Portability: Always carry fast-acting glucose (tabs/juice) in gym bag, tennis bag, and train pack.
  3. Rule of 15: At first sign of tremor/sweat/confusion: stop, take 15–20g rapid carbs, wait 15 min, retest/reassess.
  4. Creatine Pairing: Creatine (5g) taken strictly with carbs/food, never isolated on an empty stomach.
  5. Session Duration: Keep morning spin/gym to ~45–50 min to leave room for post-session breakfast.

Verdict: Fully aligned with clinical standards for managing non-diabetic/reactive and exercise-associated hypoglycemia.
