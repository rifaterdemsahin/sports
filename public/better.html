Official list from better.org.uk/leisure-centre/cambridge. A Cambridge Better membership covers these sites. Gym, pool, and class slots are bookable. Ice, lido, and splashpads may need a separate session booking even for members.

🏋️🏊 Parkside Pools and Gym

Gonville Place, Cambridge CB1 1LY

  • Gym (100-station), pool, dive pool, fitness studio, outdoor functional area
