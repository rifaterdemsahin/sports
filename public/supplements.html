Supplements & daily energy

🎂 46 yrs 📏 183 cm ⚖️ 96 kg 📊 BMI ⚠️ Hypoglycaemia — pair creatine with food

⚡ Creatine — boost the day

Creatine monohydrate, 5 g every day (including Reading, London, and rest days). It tops up muscle phosphocreatine so morning gym, spin, and tennis feel less flat. It is not a stimulant and it is not a carb — take it with food so glucose stays stable.

🧪 Daily dose

5 g creatine monohydrate in water. Same time every day. Optional load: 20 g/day split into 4×5 g for 5 days, then 5 g.

🕗 When

06:15 with the pre-session snack (🍌 banana / 🍞 toast) on gym mornings, or with breakfast on travel days. Consistency beats timing.

💧 Water

Extra ~400 ml around the dose. Creatine pulls water into muscle. Skip if you feel bloated; never take it fasted with hypo risk.

🔥 Daily calorie needs

Mifflin–St Jeor BMR for a 46-year-old man at 96 kg / 183 cm, then multiplied by activity. Stay-fit target is a small deficit on the moderate week you actually live.
