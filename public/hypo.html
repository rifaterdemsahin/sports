⚠️ Glucose first · then training

Hypoglycaemia protocol

⚠️ Do not train fasted ⚡ Creatine with food only
  1. 🍌 Eat a carb snack 15–30 min before morning sessions (banana, toast, juice).
  2. 🧃 Carry fast carbs (glucose tabs or juice) to gym, court, and trains.
  3. 🛑 If shaky, sweaty, confused, or vision changes: stop, sit, take 15–20 g fast carb, wait 15 min, reassess.
  4. 🕗 Morning spin/gym stays under an hour so you can eat breakfast after.
  5. ⚡ Creatine 5 g is taken with that snack or breakfast — never on an empty stomach.
  6. ℹ️ Stay-fit plan, not medical advice. Follow your clinician if they differ.
