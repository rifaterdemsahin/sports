    ⚠️ Hypoglycaemia protocol

    1. Eat a carb snack 15–30 min before morning sessions (banana, toast, juice). Do not train fasted.
    2. Carry fast carbs (glucose tabs or juice) to gym, court, and trains.
    3. If shaky, sweaty, confused, or vision changes: stop, sit, take 15–20 g fast carb, wait 15 min, reassess.
    4. Morning spin/gym is short (45–50 min) so you can eat breakfast after and still start the day.
    5. This is a stay-fit plan, not medical advice. Follow your clinician if they differ.
