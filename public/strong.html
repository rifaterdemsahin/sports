💪 Rule of thumb · 1 hour of sport a day · 75 days

75 Strong

⏱️ 60 minutes · one session 📅 75 days of showing up ⚠️ Not 75 Hard — no two-a-days, no restart punishment

🎯 The rule

One hour of sport every day. That is the stay-fit line for Rifat Erdem Sahin. Gym, spin, tennis, swim, or a London/Reading walk all count if the clock hits about 60 minutes and you fuel first (hypoglycaemia).

75 Hard asks for two 45-minute workouts, a gallon of water, zero alcohol, and a restart from day 1 if you miss anything. 75 Strong keeps the 75-day spine and drops the grind that would wreck a 46-year-old with hypos and Thursday trains. Miss a day: log it, start again tomorrow. Do not restart the whole 75.

📋 Daily Strong checklist

  1. ⏱️ 60 minutes of sport — one block (or two easy pieces that add to 60 on travel days).
  2. 🍌 Fuel 15–30 min before. Do not train fasted.
  3. ⚡ Creatine 5 g with food, every day including rest-shaped days.
  4. 💧 ~2–3 litres of water — extra ~400 ml around creatine. Not a forced gallon.
  5. 🔥 Stay near ~2,612 kcal on a normal week (see Supplements).

Optional: 📖 10 pages of anything, 📸 one photo if you like tracking. Neither is required to keep the streak.
